Investment Thesis

1. Structural Growth in Wires & Cables

India's infrastructure investment, power transmission, renewable energy, housing, manufacturing and data centre expansion provide a strong long-term demand environment for wires and cables.

2. Continued Market Share Gains

Polycab has steadily increased its share of India's organised wires and cables market. Its manufacturing scale, distribution network, product breadth and brand provide the company with the opportunity to continue growing faster than the overall market.

3. FMEG Margin Optionality

Polycab's FMEG segment has moved toward profitability. Continued growth combined with operating leverage could make the segment a more meaningful contributor to earnings over the next several years.

4. Export Growth

International markets remain a relatively small part of Polycab's revenue. Expansion of exports could provide a second growth engine and improve the company's overall business mix.

Business Overview

Wires & Cables

Polycab's core business and its largest source of revenue. Demand spans residential, industrial, infrastructure, renewable energy and power transmission markets.

FMEG

Includes products such as fans, lighting, switches and solar-related products. The segment represents Polycab's attempt to build a broader consumer electricals platform.

EPC

Engineering, procurement and construction operations provide additional exposure to infrastructure and electrical projects.

Comparable Companies

Sensitivity Analysis

The valuation tests changes in WACC, terminal growth and key operating assumptions to understand the range of potential outcomes.

Key Risks

Competition

Increased capacity and new entrants could place pressure on market share and pricing.

Commodity Prices

Copper and aluminium price volatility can affect pricing, channel inventory and reported revenue growth.

FMEG Execution

The investment case assumes that FMEG becomes a sustainably profitable business over time.

Valuation Risk

A premium valuation increases the downside if earnings growth or return on capital falls below expectations.
