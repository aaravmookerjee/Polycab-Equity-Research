Polycab India Equity Research & Valuation

This project is a fundamental equity research and valuation analysis of Polycab India Limited.

The objective was to understand the business in detail, build a financial model based on its key operating drivers, and arrive at a valuation using both discounted cash flow and comparable company analysis.

The analysis focuses on Polycab’s wires and cables business, its expansion into FMEG, export growth, capital allocation, and the sustainability of its long-term earnings growth.

Project Overview

Polycab is one of India’s leading wires and cables companies, with a strong presence across retail, institutional, infrastructure, and industrial markets.

Over time, the company has also expanded into fast-moving electrical goods such as fans, lighting, switches, and solar-related products.

This project looks at whether Polycab can continue to grow faster than the broader market while maintaining strong margins and returns on capital.

The key areas of focus are:

Growth in India’s wires and cables market

Continued market share gains

Expansion and profitability of the FMEG business

Growth in exports

Capital expenditure and capacity expansion

Working capital efficiency

Free cash flow generation

Return on capital

Investment Thesis

The analysis is built around four main themes.

1. Structural Growth in Wires and Cables

Demand for wires and cables in India is supported by long-term trends such as infrastructure spending, power transmission, renewable energy, housing, manufacturing, and data centre investment.

Polycab is well positioned to benefit from this growth because of its scale, manufacturing capabilities, brand strength, and distribution network.

2. Continued Market Share Gains

Polycab has steadily increased its share of the organised wires and cables market.

Its broad product range, strong dealer network, and manufacturing scale have helped it grow faster than the industry.

A major part of the valuation depends on whether Polycab can continue to outperform the market over the next several years.

3. FMEG Margin Improvement

Polycab’s FMEG business has historically been much smaller and less profitable than its core wires and cables business.

However, the segment has started moving toward profitability.

If the company can continue growing the business while improving margins, FMEG could become a more meaningful contributor to earnings over time.

4. Export Growth

Exports currently represent a relatively small part of Polycab’s revenue.

Management has identified international markets as an important area of future growth.

A higher export contribution could support revenue growth, diversify the business, and potentially improve the overall product mix.

Financial Model

The financial model includes historical financials, segment analysis, forward estimates, and valuation.

The forecast is built around the main drivers of the business rather than simply applying a fixed growth rate to revenue.

Key assumptions include:

Wires and cables industry growth

Polycab’s market share gains

Domestic volume growth

Pricing and commodity effects

Export growth

FMEG revenue growth

FMEG margin expansion

Wires and cables margins

Working capital

Capital expenditure

Tax

Free cash flow

The forecast period covers FY27E to FY31E.

Valuation

Two primary valuation methods were used.

Discounted Cash Flow

The DCF is based on Free Cash Flow to Firm.

The model includes:

Revenue and margin forecasts

Working capital assumptions

Capital expenditure

Depreciation

Tax

WACC

Terminal growth

Net cash

Sensitivity analysis

The objective was to understand how changes in operating assumptions affect the implied value of the company.

Comparable Company Analysis

Polycab was also compared with relevant listed peers, including:

Havells India

KEI Industries

RR Kabel

Finolex Cables

V-Guard Industries

The comparison looks at valuation multiples alongside differences in growth, margins, return on capital, and business mix.

Scenario Analysis

The model includes Bear, Base, and Bull cases.

Bear Case

The Bear Case assumes slower industry growth, greater competition, lower market share gains, slower FMEG margin improvement, and weaker export growth.

Base Case

The Base Case assumes Polycab continues to grow faster than the industry, improves FMEG profitability, increases exports, and maintains healthy margins in its core business.

Bull Case

The Bull Case assumes stronger market share gains, successful FMEG scaling, higher export contribution, and continued strong demand from infrastructure and industrial spending.

Key Risks

The main risks considered in the analysis are:

Increasing competition in wires and cables

Slower infrastructure or real estate activity

Copper and aluminium price volatility

Lower than expected FMEG profitability

Delays in export growth

Lower returns on planned capital expenditure

Valuation multiple compression

Research Process

The project followed this process:

Company Research → Historical Analysis → Driver Analysis → Financial Forecasting → Scenario Analysis → DCF → Comparable Companies → Investment Thesis → Equity Research Report

The main objective throughout the project was to keep the assumptions understandable and linked to the underlying business.
